Why Textron Select costs less

Select parts are the parts owners most often replace with FAA-approved copies. Textron makes or sources them in volume, sells them under the Textron name with a 1-year warranty, and prices them within a few dollars of the copy. Premium and airframe-critical parts stay in the Genuine line.
